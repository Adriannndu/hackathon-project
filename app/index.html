HeatLens

Is a heat wave coming to your part of Delhi, and how exposed is your ward when one hits? A 7-day forecast, plus a score for each ward on heat hazard, exposure, sensitivity and ability to cope.

Read this first. The ward score is a relative ranking of Delhi's wards, not a probability of anyone being harmed, and it has not been checked against heat-illness data. The heat-wave outlook applies the IMD rule to a weather-model forecast, not to official IMD warnings. Follow IMD and Delhi government alerts, and treat this as a way to ask "where should we look first?"
