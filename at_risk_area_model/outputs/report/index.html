Delhi ward heat-risk ranking (prototype)

Which wards are most at risk when a heat wave hits? Each ward is scored against the others on heat hazard, exposure, sensitivity and ability to cope.

Read this first. This is a relative ranking of Delhi's wards. It is not a forecast, not a probability of anyone being harmed, and it has not been checked against heat-illness data. Treat it as a way to ask "where should we look first?" and check the details before acting.
Dashed outline = Census-based factors were estimated for this ward (the Census has no row for it). Grey = not scored.
